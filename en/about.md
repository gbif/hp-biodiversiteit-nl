## About this portal
On Biodiversiteit-NL, you’ll find data on biodiversity from and about the Netherlands. The portal is designed to allow everyone - from researchers and citizen scientists to policymakers, conservationists, and educators - to explore biodiversity data in an accessible way. This ensures that Dutch biodiversity data from GBIF (Global Biodiversity Information Facility) can be used to its fullest potential for policy, education, and research.

--------

## Data About and From the Netherlands
On this portal, you can explore two categories of data: data about the Netherlands and data from the Netherlands.

### Data About the Netherlands
This category includes biodiversity observations in the Netherlands. Here you’ll find an overview of the distribution of animal, plant, and fungal species in the Netherlands - on land, in freshwater, and in the sea.

### Data from the Netherlands
This category includes datasets and data published by Dutch institutions, organizations, and initiatives. This data represents the Netherlands’ contribution to the global knowledge base on biodiversity both at home and abroad.

--------

## What is [GBIF](https://www.gbif.org/)?
The Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF) provides free and open access to billions of biodiversity records from around the world on a single global platform.

GBIF enables institutions, research organizations, government agencies, museums, citizen science initiatives, and other data providers to publish biodiversity observations, data from museum collections, and related information in accordance with internationally agreed-upon standards.

In this way, biodiversity data can be used worldwide for research, conservation, environmental reporting, and biodiversity monitoring.

--------

## What is [NLBIF](https://www.nlbif.nl/)?
NLBIF (Netherlands Biodiversity Information Facility) is the Dutch node of GBIF. NLBIF supports the publication, discoverability, and use of biodiversity data originating from the Netherlands.

As part of the global GBIF network, NLBIF collaborates with Dutch research institutions, natural history collections, government organizations, NGOs, and citizen science initiatives to make biodiversity data publicly available in accordance with international standards.

NLBIF assists data providers in publishing their datasets via GBIF, promotes best practices in biodiversity data management, and supports the Dutch biodiversity research and policy community in the effective use of biodiversity information.

By linking Dutch biodiversity data to the global GBIF infrastructure, NLBIF ensures that information collected in the Netherlands contributes to international biodiversity knowledge while remaining accessible for national applications.

--------

## Why GBIF?
Bringing together open and freely accessible biodiversity data, and developing the necessary tools for publishing and analyzing it, are essential for:
- Modern scientific research;
- The protection of biodiversity;
- The equitable distribution of the ecosystem services it provides;
- Technological and sustainable development; and
- Improving people’s quality of life

More information is available at [NLBIF.nl](https://www.nlbif.nl/).

--------

## How the portal uses GBIF data
The portal does not manage a separate database but uses data published through the GBIF network.

The data displayed here comes from GBIF and is organized to support Dutch biodiversity research, monitoring, reporting, and decision-making. By utilizing GBIF’s open infrastructure and international standards, the portal ensures that users have access to up-to-date, globally interconnected biodiversity information, while also benefiting from a Dutch perspective.

## [Open Data and Attribution](https://www.gbif.org/terms)
The biodiversity data accessible through this portal remains the property of the original data providers and publishers. Users should always check the licenses, citations, and attribution requirements of the datasets before reusing the data.

Use the data responsibly and correctly credit the researchers, institutions, organizations, and citizen scientists who make this data available.

GBIF, NLBIF, and the broader biodiversity community are working together to create an open and collaborative foundation for understanding and conserving biodiversity in the Netherlands and around the world.
